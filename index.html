Installation & Setup

How to Install the APK on Android

DeepFocus is an independent, open-source application. Follow these simple steps to install and configure it in less than 2 minutes.

1

Download the APK File

Tap the download button below or scan the QR code with your phone. The file is named deepfocus-debug.apk (~23 MB).

Download via GitHub Release Local Mirror Download
Direct APK Link: https://github.com/omor-com/DeepFocus/releases/download/V1.0.0/deepfocus-debug.apk
2

Allow "Install Unknown Apps"

When downloading via Google Chrome or your mobile browser, Android may show a standard notice: "File might be harmful". Tap Download anyway.

Security Tip: DeepFocus is 100% open-source and contains zero advertising SDKs or tracking code. This dialog is Android's standard warning for all APKs downloaded outside Google Play.
3

Tap Install & Google Play Protect

Open your notification bar or Downloads folder and tap deepfocus-debug.apk to initiate the package installer.

If Google Play Protect prompts with "Unrecognized app", tap "More details" and then tap "Install anyway".

Quick Guide: Once installed, tap Open to launch DeepFocus for the first time.
4

Set DeepFocus as Default Home App (Launcher)

Press your phone's Home button or swipe up. Android will ask which app to use as your Home app. Select DeepFocus and tap "Always".

Alternative: You can also go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Default Apps > Home App and select DeepFocus.
5

(Recommended) Enable Focus Guard Accessibility

To prevent bypassing locked focus sessions, open DeepFocus > swipe to Settings > tap "Enable Focus Guard Accessibility".

Why this matters: Focus Guard automatically redirects you back to your focus session if you try to open restricted distracting apps or kill the launcher while in active lock.

Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DeepFocus and APK installation.

Why does Android warn that the APK might be harmful?
Android displays this generic security warning for every file installed outside the Google Play Store (sideloaded APKs). DeepFocus is completely safe, 100% open-source, and does not contain advertisements, malware, or background tracking. You can inspect the entire source code on GitHub.
How do I switch back to my phone's default launcher if needed?
You have full control over your device at all times. When a focus session is not running, simply go to your phone's Settings > Apps > Default Apps > Home App and select your manufacturer's original launcher (One UI, Pixel Launcher, etc.). You can switch back and forth anytime without losing your habits or focus statistics!
What happens if my phone reboots during an active focus lock?
DeepFocus is engineered specifically to prevent restarting the phone to cheat the timer. The session relies on an absolute system end-timestamp rather than a fragile in-memory countdown. DeepFocus registers a BootReceiver that restores the locked session and verifies remaining time the moment the phone turns back on.
Does DeepFocus consume extra battery in the background?
Quite the opposite! Because DeepFocus uses pure deep obsidian dark colors (#070B09 and #000000), AMOLED/OLED screens turn off pixels completely on the home screen. Combined with zero background telemetry and efficient local SQLite, DeepFocus consistently extends device battery life compared to animation-heavy launchers.
Is my habit and focus data private?
100% private. DeepFocus doesn't require an account, email, or cloud login. All habit data, session records, and pinned applications are stored exclusively inside your phone's encrypted Room SQLite database. Your data never leaves your device.
